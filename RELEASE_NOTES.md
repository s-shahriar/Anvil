First release of Anvil: General and ICT quizzes in one app, working offline.

- **General**: Bangla, English, Sahitya, GK, LiveMCQ and Vocabulary quizzes, study, exams; Written data, math formulas, financial terms.
- **ICT**: MCQ, Written / Extra / Viva answers, Linux and SQL practice, equations.
- Nailed / Important / Weak, notes, text highlights and a recycle bin, synced with your account.
- Everything downloads once and then works with no connection.
- Left/right-hand layout, light/dark themes, and updates delivered inside the app.
